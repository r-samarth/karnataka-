About Karnataka

The Land of Sandalwood

Karnataka is a state in the southwestern region of India. It was formed on 1 November 1956 and is the sixth largest Indian state by area. The state capital is Bengaluru (Bangalore), which is also known as the "Silicon Valley of India".

Karnataka is known for its rich cultural heritage, diverse geography, and historical monuments. The state has a coastline stretching about 320 km along the Arabian Sea. It is home to the Western Ghats, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and has more than 25,000 temples.

  • Capital: Bengaluru
  • Language: Kannada
  • Area: 1,91,791 sq km
  • Population: ~6.7 crore (2021)
  • Known as: Karunadu (Land of Black Soil)

Famous Tourist Places

Mysore Palace

Mysore Palace is one of the most visited monuments in India. Built in 1912, it blends Hindu, Muslim, Rajput, and Gothic styles. The palace is illuminated with nearly 1 lakh light bulbs on Sundays and during Dasara.

Hampi

Hampi is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and was the capital of the Vijayanagara Empire in the 14th century. It is famous for its stunning ruins, temples, and the iconic stone chariot.

Gokarna

Gokarna is a small temple town on the western coast of India. It is known for its pristine beaches like Om Beach, Kudle Beach, and Half Moon Beach. It's a popular alternative to Goa.

Coorg (Kodagu)

Known as the "Scotland of India", Coorg is famous for its coffee plantations, lush green hills, and misty landscapes. Abbey Falls and Raja's Seat are must-visit spots.

Nandi Hills

Nandi Hills is a popular hill station near Bengaluru. Located at 1,478 meters above sea level, it is famous for its sunrise views, pleasant weather, and Tipu Sultan's Summer Palace.

Murudeshwar

Murudeshwar is famous for the world's second tallest Shiva statue (123 feet) located on the Kanduka Hill. The temple town offers beautiful views of the Arabian Sea.

Historical Places

Hampi - Vijayanagara Empire

The ruins of Hampi represent the last capital of the Vijayanagara Empire (1336-1646 AD). The stone chariot at Vittala Temple is one of India's most iconic monuments.

Badami Cave Temples

The Badami Cave Temples are a complex of Hindu and Jain temples built during the 6th century by the Chalukya dynasty. The rock-cut caves are known for their beautiful sculptures.

Belur & Halebidu

Belur and Halebidu were the capitals of the Hoysala Empire. The Chennakeshava Temple at Belur took 103 years to complete and is known for its intricate stone carvings.

Srirangapatna

Srirangapatna was the capital of Tipu Sultan, the "Tiger of Mysore". Daria Daulat Bagh (Tipu's Summer Palace) and the Gumbaz mausoleum are major attractions here.
